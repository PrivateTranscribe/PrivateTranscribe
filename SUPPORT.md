# Support

PrivateTranscribe is a small open-source project. Here is where to go.

- **Questions and setup help** go to [Discussions Q&A](https://github.com/PrivateTranscribe/PrivateTranscribe/discussions/categories/q-a). Search there first, since someone may have asked already.
- **Bugs** go in the [bug report form](https://github.com/PrivateTranscribe/PrivateTranscribe/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). A log excerpt helps us most.
- **Ideas** go to [Discussions Ideas](https://github.com/PrivateTranscribe/PrivateTranscribe/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **Security problems** go privately through the repository's **Security** tab, as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.

[WINDOWS_TROUBLESHOOTING.md](WINDOWS_TROUBLESHOOTING.md) covers common problems, and [DEBUG.md](DEBUG.md) shows how to turn on debug logs.

Everyone taking part follows our [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

We read everything, but we do not promise a response time.
